Taptiva

Remote Touch Control for Mac. Fast, private, and directly connected to your phone.

⌁

How to connect

Open Taptiva on your Mac and scan the QR code with your iPhone or Android phone. Your phone connects directly to your Mac.

⌨

Accessibility

Taptiva requires Accessibility permission to control your Mac. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security → Accessibility, and enable Taptiva.

◉

Connection problems

Make sure your Mac and phone are connected to the same Wi-Fi network. If necessary, reconnect by scanning the QR code again.

↗

Privacy

Taptiva uses a direct connection between your phone and Mac. No cloud account is required.
