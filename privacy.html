Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters.

Overview

Taptiva is a remote touch control application for Mac. It allows users to control their Mac using an iPhone or Android device over a local network.

Taptiva is designed to work without requiring an account, cloud service, or subscription.

Data Collection

Taptiva itself does not collect, store, or sell personal information through the application. The Taptiva app does not require an account or cloud service to provide its core functionality.

Our website uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the website and to help us improve its content and functionality.

Google Analytics may collect information such as approximate geographic location, browser and device information, session statistics, and interactions with the website. Google Analytics may also use first-party cookies, including the _ga cookie, to distinguish users and sessions.

We do not intentionally send personally identifiable information to Google Analytics.

Local Network Communication

Taptiva communicates directly between your Mac and your mobile device over your local network.

Information exchanged during this connection is used solely to provide the remote control functionality of the application.

Accessibility Permission

Taptiva requires macOS Accessibility permission to perform remote control actions on your Mac, such as interacting with the mouse, keyboard, and other supported system controls.

This permission is managed by macOS and can be revoked at any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility.

Third-Party Services

Our website uses Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google LLC.

Google Analytics is used to measure website usage and understand how visitors interact with Taptiva's website. We do not use Google Analytics for advertising or remarketing unless this Privacy Policy is updated to reflect such use.

Google Analytics may use cookies and other identifiers to provide its measurement services. The use of these technologies may be subject to applicable privacy and consent requirements depending on the visitor's location.

Taptiva does not use third-party advertising services on this website.

Children's Privacy

Taptiva does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
